## 9. Notes for Task 04 (grid contract)

- Source-grid origin: the crop starts at source pixel col0=633, row0=1840
  (0-based indices in the ORIGINAL 4456x5067 grid).
- Row 0 = north preserved: crop row 0 is source row 1840; rows still run
  north → south and pixels stay row-major (no flip, no transposition).
- Actual extent: 1016·20 m = 20320.0 m (x) × 1075·20 m = 21500.0 m (y),
  vs PLAN §5.1's "≈20×21 km" phrasing (the outward snap slightly exceeds the
  requested frame on every side).
- Pixel sizes: 20.0 m (x) × 20.0 m (y) in PROJECTED meters.
  The ground-scale x meters at lat 18.5° (≈ 19.99606821438402 m/px) is a
  grid_meta.json decision still open from Task 02 §9 item 2 — not ruled here.
- NoData sentinel and NaN counts are recorded in §8 check (h); the crop
  preserves them exactly.
