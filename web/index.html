About Jezero Safe Walk

NASA Space Apps Cairo 2026

Challenge: Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map

Team: FALCONS EGYPT

This tool is a Mission Planner for the first human astronauts on Mars. It helps plan safe Extravehicular Activities (EVAs) in Jezero Crater by comparing the "Safest" route against the "Shortest" route based on the astronaut's suit profile.

Inspiration: We were inspired by NASA's proposed path for the Curiosity rover up Mount Sharp, which features labeled science targets and route analysis.

Data Sources & Limitations

Provenance (Data Sources)

  • Terrain Elevation (DEM 20m): USGS / NASA (M20 Jezero Crater CTXDEM)
  • Rover Path: NASA PDS (Perseverance PLACES dataset)
  • Slope & Routing: Computed via Horn 3x3 method from DEM
  • Geologic Map: USGS-NASA Planetary Geologic Mapping (P2 Layer)
  • Science Targets: NASA Mars Trek
  • Thermal Data: Cited from NASA M20 First Weather Report (Archival)

Tool Limitations

  • Planning Tool Only: This is a preliminary EVA planning tool, not a real-time navigation system.
  • Suit Thresholds: The Safe/Hard/Forbidden slope thresholds are based on Perseverance rover data (99% under 16°). They are adjustable and not medically validated for humans.
  • Resolution: The 20m DEM does not show small rocks or hazards.
  • Geologic Map Offset: The optional USGS geologic map layer has a documented positional offset of ~100-200m relative to the CTX basemap.
  • Walking Speed: Estimated time assumes a constant speed (default 2 km/h) on all terrain.

Scientific Validation

Our terrain slope calculations were validated against actual Perseverance rover pitch telemetry over 32,003 points.

Disclaimer: The data pertains to the Perseverance rover, not human measurements. Validation results do not guarantee human route safety.
